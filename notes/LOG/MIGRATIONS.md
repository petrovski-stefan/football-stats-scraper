## 2026-07-26

- **Module renames.** The old `constants.py` split into `scrapers/constants.py` (DOM selectors, user agents, transliteration table) and `domain/columns.py` (`ORDERED_DF_COLUMNS`, `DETAIL_ORDERED_COLUMNS`, `per_match_columns()`).
