- **Format added after two outside reviews the user ran (2026-10-04):** every structural card
  has a Basis line: proof (true for every setting of the model), grid check (true for the

  second, independent calculation before it goes in. The summary table gives each claim its
  own row with exactly one basis (proof, grid check, example, or assumption for the model
  itself); a grid-check row starts "In the checked settings", and an example row names the
  example.

### 9 — ML / deep learning

- ML combines weak signals better, but it cannot create signal the inputs lack. RESULTS §3:
  even the market beats a constant on the half-time state by only 0.64%.
- At ~100k tabular matches, gradient boosting (e.g. LightGBM) is the realistic tool. Deep
  learning rarely beats it at this size; sequence models are worth a look only for in-play
  timelines.
- The evaluation rules (fresh data for any tested claim) stay the same.

### 13 — Audit definitions and the workflow

- **Open from the first list** (its other parts were closed on 2026-10-11, `notes/MIGRATIONS.md`):
  - **F1's 8% bar as a test.** Only 24 of the 73 leagues reach 600 matches in their two
    seasons (median 480), so 49 can never pass it. At this size its false-positive risk is
    small: 1–2.5% that at least one of the 24 passes under a pure null at 4.93–5.15%. The
    raw rate it uses mostly follows goals per match: league differences p = 5e-6 raw, 0.07
    against the odds model (2026-10-09 scan, which covered 52 of the 73 leagues; the 15
    Asian and 6 American leagues scraped later that day were not in it). There, no league
    with 600+ matches reached 8% (best 6.84%). Decide: keep it as retry #8's test, or keep
    per-league rates as description only;
  - **the weekday outside Europe.** S2.08–10 use the Berlin day. In the Americas the local
    day differs for 2,639 of 4,808 matches (55%; Mexico 98%), and 404 of the 501 Berlin
    Mondays there are Sunday evenings locally. Europe and Asia: 0 differences (kickoff
    times and rounds only, 2026-10-11). Decide: compute the weekday in each league's local
    time (storage stays Berlin);
  - **out of round: the corrected flag.** The ordinal rule misfires (PLAN_2026-09-20
    erratum 1). Closest to the user's own definition (moved, then played after a later
    round had started): ordinal **and** `s3_is_rescheduled` (S3.10) **and** played late
    (S3.09 > 0). It flags 19 of the 89 in the 4 seasons checked. Decide whether it replaces
    S3.07.
- **The workflow question:** a claim formed by looking at data needs fresh data to test it.
  That rule comes from statistics, not ML. But descriptive questions (base rates, league
  tables) need no split at all. The audit sorts each claim into descriptive, tested or
  predictive, and assigns the matching workflow.
- Also rename the ML-flavoured terms (dev/test/holdout) where a plain one exists
  (exploratory / confirmatory).
